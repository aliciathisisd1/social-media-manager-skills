# Social Media Manager — Project Guide

This project supports two clients only:

1. **Amazon**
2. **Core42**

Keep everything simple. Do not add extra clients, platforms, or workflows.

## Clients

### 1. Amazon
Content focus: short-form video about unique or quirky Amazon Finds.

Workflow (in order):
1. Find unique or quirky Amazon Finds products.
2. Include both Amazon UAE and Amazon KSA product links.
3. Shortlist products for selection.
4. Create hook and script ideas.
5. Plan the shoot.
6. Upload raw footage.
7. Edit content.
8. Send for client approval.
9. Approve and schedule.

Related skills:
- `amazon-finds-sourcing` — steps 1–3
- `amazon-video-ideation` — step 4
- `amazon-content-calendar` — steps 5, 8, 9
- `production-tracker` — steps 5–9 (tracking)

### 2. Core42
Content focus: monthly LinkedIn content (brand, leadership, employee voice).

Workflow (in order):
1. Create a monthly LinkedIn content calendar.
2. Include brand, leadership, and employee voice content.
3. Track content from idea to approval to scheduling.
4. Create reporting and insight summaries.

Related skills:
- `core42-content-calendar` — steps 1–3
- `production-tracker` — step 3 (tracking)
- `core42-reporting-engine` — step 4

## Rules

- Only work on Amazon or Core42. Ask before adding anything else.
- Always follow the workflow order for each client.
- For Amazon products, always include both UAE and KSA links.
- For Core42, always cover brand, leadership, and employee voice.
- Keep outputs short, clear, and actionable.
