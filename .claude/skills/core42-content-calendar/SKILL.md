# Core42 Content Calendar

Use this skill for Core42 workflow steps 1–3: monthly LinkedIn planning, covering the three content pillars, and tracking to approval and scheduling.

## Content pillars (must all be covered)

1. **Brand voice** — company news, products, milestones, culture.
2. **Leadership voice** — posts from or about executives / thought leaders.
3. **Employee voice** — team spotlights, employee stories, day-in-the-life.

## What to do

1. Build a monthly LinkedIn calendar.
2. Balance the three pillars across the month (aim for a roughly even split).
3. For each post, draft: topic, angle, voice, format, caption draft.
4. Move each post through: Idea → Drafted → Approved → Scheduled.

## Calendar format

| Date | Pillar | Voice (Brand / Leadership / Employee) | Topic | Format (text / image / video / carousel) | Caption draft | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ... | ... | ... | ... | ... | ... | Idea / Drafted / Approved / Scheduled / Posted |

## Rules

- Every month must include all three voices.
- Posts should fit LinkedIn's professional tone.
- Never schedule before approval.
- Keep captions clear and human — no buzzword overload.
