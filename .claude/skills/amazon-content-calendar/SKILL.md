# Amazon Content Calendar

Use this skill for Amazon workflow steps 5, 8, and 9: shoot planning, client approval, and scheduling.

## What to do

1. **Plan the shoot**
   - Group selected products into a single shoot day where possible.
   - List: date, location, products, props, talent.

2. **Send for client approval**
   - Package the edited video + caption + hashtags.
   - Clearly mark status as "Pending approval".

3. **Approve and schedule**
   - Once approved, assign a posting date, time, and platform.
   - Update status to "Scheduled".

## Calendar entry format

| Product | Shoot Date | Post Date | Platform | Caption | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ... | ... | ... | Instagram / TikTok | ... | Idea / Shot / Edited / Pending / Scheduled / Posted |

## Rules

- One status per item at a time.
- Do not schedule anything before client approval.
- Always include UAE + KSA links in the caption.
- Keep the calendar simple — one row per video.
