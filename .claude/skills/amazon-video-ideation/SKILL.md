# Amazon Video Ideation (Shoot Pack)

Use this skill for Amazon workflow step 4. Produce one Shoot Pack per product.

A Shoot Pack is a self-contained brief. A single in-house creator should read it and shoot with no questions. Prioritise visual-first storytelling.

## Shoot Pack format (MANDATORY)

Every output must include these 6 sections, in this order.

**1. Hook**
One hook, written out exactly, taken from the approved hooks list in `.claude/skills/production-tracker/SKILL.md`. No "curiosity hook" shorthand. No custom hooks unless the Social Manager has approved one.

**2. Concept (1 line)**
One plain-English sentence describing what happens in the video.

**3. Shot list (3 to 5 shots max)**
Numbered. One line per shot. Clear enough to film without a second briefing.

**4. Filming notes**
Lighting, angle, and setting. 2 to 4 bullets.

**5. Creator instruction**
How the creator should perform it. Delivery, pacing, facial expression, where to speak, where to stay silent.

**6. Ending / payoff**
The final beat. Must tie back to the hook and leave the viewer satisfied or surprised. Must mention UAE and KSA availability.

## Performance optimization rules (apply to every video)

1. **First shot creates confusion or tension.** No context, no explanation. Make the viewer ask "what is that?"
2. **Never open on a static product shot.** No packaging. No logo. No talking head intro.
3. **Include at least one interaction moment.** A touch, push, press, poke, or reveal. Motion that the eye locks onto.
4. **Include a pattern interrupt.** A sudden angle change, a cut to time-lapse, a drop, a flip. Something that breaks the rhythm before the viewer scrolls.
5. **Ending must feel satisfying or surprising.** Reveal, payoff, or callback to the hook. Never a flat outro.
6. **Do not over-explain.** Let the visuals carry the story. If the shot already shows it, do not say it.

## Example output

**Hook:** pause for a second

**Concept:** A plant floats and spins on a desk with no visible support.

**Shot list:**
1. Extreme close-up of the plant spinning mid-air. No context. Text on screen: "pause for a second".
2. Creator's finger pokes the floating pot. It wobbles, keeps spinning.
3. Hand lifts the whole pot off the base. Plant drops into hand. Hand places it back. It re-locks mid-air.
4. Wide desk shot. Laptop, coffee, plant hovering beside them.
5. Plant still spinning. Price on screen. "UAE + KSA, link below."

**Filming notes:**
- Natural side light from a window. No overhead light.
- Shoot vertical. Camera at desk height, level with the pot.
- Clean background. Nothing competing for attention.

**Creator instruction:**
- Stay silent for the first 2 seconds. Let the visual confuse the viewer.
- Poke the pot on a music beat.
- Straight face through the lift-and-replace. No reaction. Let the viewer react.

**Ending / payoff:** Plant re-locks in mid-air after the lift. Closes the loop on the hook: the viewer finally sees why they were asked to pause.

## Rules

- One Shoot Pack per product. Fits on one screen.
- Use only hooks from the approved list, written out in full.
- Shot list stays between 3 and 5 shots.
- Filming notes must be doable with a phone and natural light.
- Every ending mentions UAE and KSA.
- Tone: casual, confident, built for Reels, TikTok, and Shorts.
