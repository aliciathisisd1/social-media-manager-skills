# Amazon Video Ideation (Shoot Pack)

Use this skill for Amazon workflow step 4. Produce one Shoot Pack per product.

A Shoot Pack is a self-contained brief. A single content creator should be able to read it and shoot with no back-and-forth.

## Shoot Pack format

Every output must include these 5 sections, in this order.

**1. Hook**
One hook only, taken from the approved hooks list in `.claude/skills/production-tracker/SKILL.md`. No custom hooks unless the Social Manager has approved one (mark as `Custom (approved)`).

**2. Concept (1 line)**
One plain-English sentence describing what happens in the video.

**3. Shot list (3 to 5 shots max)**
Numbered list, one line per shot. Each shot must be clear enough to film without a second briefing.

**4. Filming notes**
Short notes on lighting, angle, and setting. 2 to 4 bullet points.

**5. Ending / payoff moment**
The final beat the viewer is left with. One line. Must mention UAE and KSA availability.

## Example output

**Hook:** Curiosity hook ("Wait, how does that even work?")

**Concept:** A plant appears to float and spin on a regular desk, no wires, no trick.

**Shot list:**
1. Close-up of the plant spinning in mid-air, no context.
2. Hand drops the pot above the magnetic base, it catches.
3. Wide desk shot: laptop, coffee, floating plant.
4. Time-lapse of the plant spinning for a full minute of work.
5. Product on desk with price on screen.

**Filming notes:**
- Natural side light from a window. Avoid overhead light, it kills the illusion.
- Shoot vertical. Camera at desk height, level with the pot.
- Keep the background clean. No clutter behind the plant.

**Ending / payoff:** Slow zoom on the floating plant with price on screen. Caption: "UAE + KSA, link below."

## Rules

- One Shoot Pack per product. Fits on one screen.
- Use only hooks from the approved list.
- Shot list stays between 3 and 5 shots. No more.
- Filming notes must be simple enough for a solo creator with a phone.
- Every ending mentions UAE and KSA.
- Tone: casual, friendly, built for Reels, TikTok, and Shorts.
