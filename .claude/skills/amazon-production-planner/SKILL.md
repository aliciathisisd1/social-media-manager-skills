# Amazon Production Planner

Purpose: turn approved ideas into shoot-ready briefs. Third step. Cannot run without ideas.

## Input

A list of approved content ideas. Each must have hook, product, concept, shot type, category.

## Output

One Production Brief per idea, with these sections:

**1. Hook** — exact wording from the approved list.
**2. Concept** — one sentence.
**3. Shot list** — 3 to 5 numbered shots, one line each.
**4. Filming notes** — 2 to 4 bullets on light, angle, and setting.
**5. Creator instruction** — delivery, pacing, where to stay silent, where to react.
**6. Ending / payoff** — final beat. Must mention UAE and KSA.

## Rules

- Must be shootable by 1 creator with a phone.
- First shot must create curiosity or tension. No static product opens.
- Product must appear within the first 2 seconds.
- Include at least one interaction moment per brief (touch, press, pour, reveal).
- Use natural light and basic framing. No studio.
- Real environment. Don't over-clean before filming.

## Amazon Now briefs

- POV framing.
- Show problem first, then 15-minute solution.
- Real, in-the-moment feel.

## Do not include

- Edit instructions
- Captions
- Client-facing summaries

## Hand-off

After shooting, raw footage feeds `amazon-edit-handoff`.
