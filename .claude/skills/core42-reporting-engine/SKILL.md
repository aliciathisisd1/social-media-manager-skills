# Core42 Reporting Engine

Use this skill for Core42 workflow step 4: reporting and insight summaries.

## What to do

Produce a short monthly report that covers:

1. **Top-line metrics**
   - Impressions
   - Engagement (likes, comments, shares)
   - Engagement rate
   - Follower growth

2. **Performance by pillar**
   - Brand voice
   - Leadership voice
   - Employee voice
   - Which pillar performed best and why.

3. **Top 3 posts**
   - Title / topic
   - Metric highlights
   - Why it worked (1 sentence).

4. **Bottom 3 posts**
   - Title / topic
   - Metric highlights
   - Why it underperformed (1 sentence).

5. **Insights & recommendations**
   - 3–5 clear takeaways.
   - 3 actions for next month.

## Report format

- Title: `Core42 LinkedIn Report — [Month Year]`
- Sections in the order above.
- Keep it to one page where possible.

## Rules

- Always split performance by the three voices (brand, leadership, employee).
- Always end with clear next-month actions.
- Do not pad with filler — short and useful beats long.
